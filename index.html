NOT JUST MODELS. SYSTEMS THAT WORK.

I’m a Computer Science & Engineering student specialising in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Ramrao Adik Institute of Technology, D. Y. Patil University. I’m focused on building practical AI products and strengthening the research behind them.

My work spans computer vision, audio AI and language models, alongside the systems around them: data preparation, model evaluation, inference APIs, web interfaces and deployment. I care about measurable performance, reproducibility and making models useful beyond a notebook.

Research interests include computer vision, image processing, deep learning, vision transformers, multimodal AI, NLP, efficient machine learning and knowledge distillation.

SLM–DistilBERT candidate-selection study

SLM–DistilBERT candidate-selection study

The study explores whether a smaller language model can reduce expensive candidate-selection or tool-use overhead while preserving downstream decision quality. Two strategies are being evaluated: direct replacement and hybrid shortlisting. The academic manuscript is in preparation.

Status: ongoing research · manuscript in preparation · no publication claimed
